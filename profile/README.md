## 🌌 Our Mission

We believe advanced AI should be more open, accessible, and collaborative.

**169pi** is building open reasoning models from India and bringing developers, researchers, students, and AI enthusiasts together to experiment, create, and push the boundaries of open-source intelligence.

We're not just building models. We're building an ecosystem where ideas become experiments, experiments become projects, and contributions become part of something bigger.

- 🧠 **Open reasoning** — Explore models designed for complex problem-solving.
- 🔬 **Research & experimentation** — Learn from benchmarks, experiments, and real-world applications.
- 🌍 **Community-driven AI** — Help shape the tools and projects we build.
- 🇮🇳 **Built in India** — Contributing to the global open-source AI ecosystem.

## 🧠 Meet Alpie-Core

<p align="center">
  <a href="https://github.com/169Pi/Alpie-Core">
    <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/ALPIE--CORE-Open%20Reasoning-7c3aed?style=for-the-badge" alt="Alpie-Core" />
  </a>
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Parameters-32B-blue?style=for-the-badge" alt="32B parameters" />
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Quantization-4--bit-green?style=for-the-badge" alt="4-bit quantization" />
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/License-Apache--2.0-orange?style=for-the-badge" alt="Apache 2.0 license" />
</p>

**Alpie-Core** is our first-generation reasoning model, built to explore challenging reasoning tasks and make experimentation with open models more accessible.

| Specification | Details |
|:--|:--|
| Model parameters | 32 billion |
| Quantization | 4-bit |
| Context length | 65K tokens |
| Reported VRAM requirement | Approximately 16 GB |
| License | Apache 2.0 |

### 📊 Reported benchmark results

| Benchmark | Reported score |
|:--|--:|
| GSM8K | **92.75%** |
| MMLU | **81.28%** |
| SWE-Bench Verified | **57.8%** |

*Benchmark figures are the reported results for the model. See the model repository for evaluation details, configurations, and reproducibility information.*

Represent your college coding club, campus community, or local meetup!

Your contribution can then count toward your club on the [Clubs Leaderboard](https://goodfirst.alpie.ai/leaderboard). The top club can win prizes according to the program rules.

## 🤝 How to Contribute

1. **Explore** — Visit the [169pi organization](https://github.com/169Pi) and choose a model, capability, or AI concept.
2. **Create** — Build an original visual, diagram, demo, or other showcase contribution.
3. **Fork** — Fork this repository and add your entry inside the designated markers.
4. **Attribute** — End your entry with `**Contributed by [@handle](profile URL)**`.
5. **Submit** — Open a pull request following the [contribution guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

Please keep your changes inside the designated entry markers. The automated checks validate contribution formatting and attribution.
